About the Archive

A brief overview of the project, its purpose, and the people behind it.

Mission and Vision

The Thesis Archive System is a centralized, local network repository designed exclusively for the students, faculty, and librarians of Cavite State University - Imus Campus.

Its primary goal is to digitize and streamline the process of browsing, borrowing, and submitting academic research projects. By transitioning from physical hardbound copies to a secure digital environment, it ensures intellectual property protection while making vital academic literature readily accessible to the campus community.
